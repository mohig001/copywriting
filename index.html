How I work

01

Understand

I start by getting to know the whats and whys of my client's project inside out through questionnaire, conversation and research.

This not only gives me the insight I need to write the best possible copy, but is often a moment where new understandings and ideas emerge for the client also.

02

Write

If the foundational questions about an idea are thoroughly answered, that is most of the work done. From here, the words write themselves.

03

Deliver

For me, good copy doesn't come from clever words or punchy taglines, but emerges when a concept is deeply thought through with clear intentionality. I believe the best writing is the most simple and the most honest.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I need a copywriter?+
We are often so immersed in our own work that it's hard to see it objectively anymore. A copywriter is an outside pair of eyes to reflect back things you might have missed, offer new insights and refreshing perspectives. A good copywriter won't just work on assumption or intuition, but will take decisions backed by research; talking to your clients, conducting market research and thoughtfully-tailored questionnaires. Reaching a thorough and grounded understanding of your work is not only beneficial for communicating with your clients, but also will leave you with more confidence and clarity in your work.
Can't I just use AI?+
Although it seems like a life hack in the short run, I think cutting corners when it comes to labouring over our ideas doesn't pay off in the big picture. I am a believer in the messy process of questioning and thinking, and that going through this struggle is how we develop work that has true integrity and originality. I am here to go through that challenging process together! A good text communicates your spirit and values not only in the words chosen, but in how those words are delivered. That's why I help you find a personal tone and style that uniquely embodies you, rather than the generic style of most AI generated text today.
What do you need from me?+

When I start working with a new client, on a website, a workshop concept or other project, I usually ask for the following things:

  • Examples of previous or existing copy (from websites or other material) so I can get an idea of the voice and style you are already using.
  • More info about yourself, organisation or business (usually from a website or CV).
  • An outline of the project you need copy for. This can be a messy and unformulated document of thoughts. In fact, I prefer to receive a document written in your own, unedited voice than something that has already passed through ChatGPT.
  • I send you a questionnaire to fill out with questions about the intention and goal of your project, the tone you are aiming for and the practicalities the text needs to fulfill.

Then I get started!

How long does it take?+

I usually send you a first draft within a few days, you tell me what you like or don't like, and from there I create the final copy.

A full website copy takes a little longer, usually between 2-3 weeks.
