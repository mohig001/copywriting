About me

As an interdisciplinary artist, musician and writer I've had to traverse and learn the language of many different fields - the art world, academic world, cultural sector, NGOs, wellness, design, entrepreneurship.

This has taught me to interpret and translate between worlds, but also given me an acute allergy towards all things lingo and jargon.

Because, personally, I would rather read a text that is honest and tries to reveal the essence of things, than something witty or attention-grabbing.

So this is also what I try to write.

I bring the same curiosity and love for questions to copywriting that I do to making a film or a song because the fundamental questions are the same:

What are we actually trying to say here? And why?

I would love to think along with you on your next project and help you communicate your vision in a way that deeply resonates.
