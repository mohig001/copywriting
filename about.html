Hi, I'm Miche!

Although I call myself a writer, the writing itself is never what I care about most. I care more about the thinking behind it and the conditions needed for good ideas to emerge. Because I believe we need them more than ever to face the many crises we are living through.

But even the best ideas are rarely enough on their own - they need a well crafted body to be able to move. For me writing is a vessel that can carry our thoughts into the world. I aspire to write words that make people and structures move. That are sticky and contagious and irresistible, so that our ideas can travel and take root outside of ourselves.

I am the copywriter for people who want to do more than just sell. For people who share the feeling that we need something else in the world right now, even if we are not sure what that something else is yet. For people who believe we need to try anyway.

I would love to help you weave some beautiful words for your business or project.
