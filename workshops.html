Workshops and Mentorship

Organisations, Schools, Festivals and Individuals

I love getting to share my passion for writing processes through workshops that show how playful and diverse writing can be. They vary from the more experimental to very practical (often both combined) but in all of them, I aim to leave people feeling empowered to keep writing in a way and a voice that fits them. Explore my current and past workshops.

I also offer 1-1 writing mentorship for personal or professional writing projects. I offer support getting a writing project started and planned out as well as offering accountability, regular check-ins, writing advice and proofreading.
